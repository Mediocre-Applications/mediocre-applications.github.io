What it does

Fully private

No ads. No analytics. No tracking. Everything stays on your device.

Pinned apps & groups

Pin the apps you actually use and organise them into named groups. Long-press any of them for its shortcuts.

Search that finds everything

Apps, contacts and app shortcuts as you type — with a calculator and unit conversion built in.

Calendar & media cards

Today’s events and whatever’s playing, right on the home screen — each card turns up only when there is something to show. Recent apps can sit there too.

Clock, your way

Five clock styles — Default, Giant, Vivid, Flip and Analog — or hand the job to any clock widget you like.

Contacts on the home screen

Pin the people you actually talk to. Call or message them in a tap, or find them from search.

Gestures

Map swipes (down, left, right) and drawn gestures (circle, Z) to an app, search, the notification shade, quick settings, the assistant or the lock screen.

App stats

Screen time, opens, daily averages and streaks for every app — worked out on your device from Android’s own usage data.

Icons, your way

Five icon shapes, third-party icon packs, the system themed-icon layer, or a custom icon for a single app. Optional notification dots.

Themes & fonts

System, Light or Dark, and your pick of font throughout the launcher.

Your wallpaper, showing

Turn the background transparency down to let more of your wallpaper through.

Hidden apps

Long-press anything you’d rather not look at and it leaves the list. Still installed, just out of the way.

Backup & restore

Save your whole setup to a file and put it back on a new phone.

Delete your data

NEON HALO: Rise of the Jumpbot 5000, by Mediocre Applications

Last updated: 14 August 2026 · Privacy policy

There is no account to delete. NEON HALO has no accounts and no servers of ours — your progress is a file on your phone. Depending on what you want removed, there are three routes, and two of them you can do yourself right now.

Step 1 — Your save file, settings and progress

You can do this yourself · takes effect immediately

Everything the game remembers — best height, lifetime statistics, unlocked chassis, salvaged alloy, gear stash, settings — is stored only in the app’s private storage on your device. Deleting it is immediate and needs no request:

  1. Uninstall the game, or
  2. open Settings → Apps → NEON HALO → Storage on your Android device and tap Clear storage.

Either removes all of it permanently. We hold no copy — it was never sent to us, so there is nothing on our side to delete and nothing we could restore.

Step 2 — Leaderboard score, gamertag and achievements

Only if you signed in to Google Play Games · you can do this yourself

Signing in to Google Play Games Services is optional, and most of this section does not apply if you declined. If you did sign in, your best height, your Play Games gamertag and your unlocked achievements are held by Google, not by us. Delete them through your Google account:

  1. Open the Google Play Games app.
  2. Go to More → Settings.
  3. Choose Delete Play Games account and data, then pick NEON HALO to delete this game’s data only — or delete your whole Play Games profile.

The same controls are at myaccount.google.com. We cannot delete this for you: we never receive your email address, your real name or your Google account identifier, only the opaque per-game player ID Google issues, so we have no way to find or remove your entry.

On a future iOS version the equivalent is Game Center, deleted from Settings → [your name] → Game Center on your device.

Step 3 — Crash reports

Email us · we reply within 30 days

When the game crashes, a diagnostic report goes to Sentry, our crash reporting provider. To request deletion of crash reports:

  1. Email anton@adamsen.io with the subject “Data deletion request — NEON HALO”.
  2. Include anything that helps us find the report: roughly when the crash happened, your device model, and the app version (Settings → DIAGNOSTICS in the game).

We will delete any report we can identify from what you give us, and reply to tell you what we found. Crash reports carry no name, email or account, so there is often nothing that ties one to a particular person — if we cannot identify a report as yours, we will say so plainly rather than guess. Every crash report is deleted automatically after 90 days in any case.

What is deleted, what is kept

Data Held by How it is deleted Retention
Save file, statistics and settings Your device only Step 1 — uninstall or clear storage Deleted immediately; never held by us
Leaderboard score, gamertag, achievements Google Play Games Services Step 2 — your Google account Kept by Google until you delete it; we cannot
Crash reports (error, device model, OS version, app version) Sentry, on our behalf Step 3 — email request Deleted automatically after 90 days regardless
Purchase record for the game Google Play Not deletable — Google keeps it as a record of sale Per Google’s retention, in your Google Play account

There is nothing else. We operate no backend and hold no database of players, so beyond the crash reports above there is no account, profile or record of you on our side to delete. We never collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera input, or location.

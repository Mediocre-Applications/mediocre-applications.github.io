NEON HALO

Rise of the Jumpbot 5000

A pixel-art endless vertical jumper for Android. You play a scavenged mech auto-bouncing up an endless column of platforms; tilt the phone to steer. Climb through seven altitude zones — the rain and refinery flares of THE SPRAWL, up past the derelict skyline, out through orbit and the breaking yards, into the neon halo itself. Fall off the bottom and it’s over.

01 // How it plays

Three rules, and that’s the game.

No tutorial. Press start and go.

RULE 01

NO JUMP BUTTON

The bounce is automatic. Tilt to aim it, or switch to touch controls and drag with a thumb. Sensitivity and dead zone are yours to set.

RULE 02

LAND ON ITS HEAD

Machines hover in your way. Come down on top of one and it bursts into scrap. Hit it any other way and the run ends.

RULE 03

THERE IS NO TOP

The column is built as you climb and it doesn’t run out. The only way to lose is the bottom of the screen.

04 // What you keep

Alloy buys everything here.

None of it costs real money.

CURRENCY

ALLOY

Chunks spawn in the lane and scrapped machines drop more. It banks as you climb, so quitting a run doesn’t cost you what you picked up.

GEAR

FOUR THINGS TO CARRY

A rocket, a hover field for picking your landing, a coil that overcharges your next few bounces, and a shield. Find them in the column or buy them at the scrap market.

CHASSIS

TWELVE TO UNLOCK

Some come with reaching a new sky. The rest take the kind of run that needs practice.

PROPERTY

A ROOM HIGHER UP

Buy an apartment further up the column and the next run starts on its doorstep. You’re paying to skip a climb you’ve already done.

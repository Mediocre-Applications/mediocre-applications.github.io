Summary

No account · No backend · No ads

NEON HALO: Rise of the Jumpbot 5000 (iOS bundle identifier dk.antontanderup.neonhalo) is a single-player arcade game published by Mediocre Applications (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what the game does with data. The short version: there is no account to create, there are no servers of ours for the game to talk to, and your progress lives on your phone.

We operate no backend. Everything described below that leaves your device goes to Apple (as the app store and, if you choose to sign in, as the leaderboard and achievements host) or to Sentry (crash diagnostics). We hold no database of players, and there is no server we could look you up in. NEON HALO is a paid app, and there is no advertising.

1. What stays on your device

Your save file — best height, lifetime statistics, unlocked chassis, salvaged alloy, gear stash and every settings choice — is written to the app’s own private storage on your phone and never transmitted anywhere. It is not backed up to us, it is not synced between devices, and it cannot be recovered by us if it is lost.

Uninstalling the game, or offloading it from iOS’s app settings, deletes all of it permanently.

The game requests no runtime permissions. It reads the device’s motion sensors (the gravity sensor, to steer by tilt), which on iOS is not a permission-gated capability and which we neither record nor transmit — the reading is used for the current frame and discarded.

2. Crash diagnostics (Sentry)

When the game crashes or hits an unexpected error, a diagnostic report is sent to Sentry, which we use to find and fix bugs. A report contains the error and its stack trace, the app version, the device model, the operating system version, and coarse technical context about what the app was doing. Sentry also records the IP address the report arrived from as part of delivering it.

We use these reports for one purpose — diagnosing faults in the game — and we do not attempt to identify individual players from them. Reports are retained for up to 90 days on Sentry’s default retention and then deleted. Nothing about how you play, what you buy, or how far you climb is included.

3. Apple platform services

Distribution is an Apple product, and if you choose to sign in, so are leaderboards and achievements. Apple’s own handling of that data is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy, not by this one.

3.1 Installation and updates

The App Store tells us nothing that identifies you. We can see aggregate, anonymised statistics in App Store Connect — install counts, crash rates, country totals, device breakdowns — and any review or rating you choose to write publicly. Purchasing the game itself is handled entirely by the App Store; your payment details are never seen by the app or by us.

3.2 Leaderboards and achievements (Game Center)

Applies to versions of the game that offer leaderboards and achievements. Signing in is optional, and the game is fully playable without it.

If you choose to sign in to Game Center, your best height is submitted to a public leaderboard hosted by Apple, alongside the Game Center nickname and avatar you have chosen there. Anyone viewing the leaderboard can see that nickname, that avatar and that score. Signing in also lets the game unlock Game Center achievements for milestones you reach (such as reaching a new zone or unlocking a chassis); which achievements you have unlocked is likewise visible on your Game Center profile. We receive no email address, no real name and no Apple ID — only the opaque player ID Apple issues for this game.

You can decline to sign in, sign out at any time, control the visibility of your Game Center profile, and delete your Game Center data for this game, from the Game Center app or from Settings → [your name] → Game Center on your device.

4. What we never collect

The game does not ask for and does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera input, or precise location. It has no analytics SDK tracking how you play, and no advertising of any kind.

5. Children

NEON HALO is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through this app, contact us at the address below and we will act on it.

You have the right to access, correct, erase and object to the processing of your personal data, and to complain to your national data protection authority. Because we hold no database of players, the practical route to exercising most of these rights is through the device and Apple ID account controls listed above — but write to us and we will help.

7. Changes to this policy

When the game gains a feature that changes what data is involved, this page is updated in the same release and the date at the top changes with it. The history of every change to this page is public, commit by commit, in the repository that serves it.

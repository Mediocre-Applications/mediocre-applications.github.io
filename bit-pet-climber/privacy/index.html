Privacy Policy — Google Play

Bit Pet Climber · Last updated: 1 November 2026

Summary

No account · No backend · No ads

Bit Pet Climber (Android package dk.antontanderup.bitpetclimber) is a single-player game published by Mediocre Applications (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what the game does with data. There is no account to create, there are no servers of ours for the game to talk to, and your progress lives on your phone, plus a copy in your own Google account if you choose to sign in.

We operate no backend. Everything described below that leaves your device goes to Google (as the app store, the payment processor and, if you sign in, the host of the leaderboard, the achievements and the cloud save) or to Sentry (crash diagnostics). We hold no database of players, and there is no advertising.

1. What stays on your device

Your save file, which holds your progress, your pets and the names you give them, your in-game balance and items, what you have bought, and your settings, is written to the app’s own private storage on your phone. It is never sent to us, and we cannot recover it for you if it is lost.

If you sign in to Google Play Games Services, a copy of your progress is also kept in your own Google account. Section 3.3 describes it. Otherwise nothing in the save leaves your phone.

Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage from Android’s app settings, permanently deletes the copy on the phone.

The game reads the device’s motion sensors to steer by tilt. That reading is used for the current frame and discarded; it is neither recorded nor transmitted.

2. Notifications

If you switch on reminders, the game asks for Android’s notification permission and schedules reminders on your phone. They are created and delivered locally: no push service is used and nothing is sent to us. You can turn them off in the game’s settings or in Android’s app settings at any time.

3. Google Play services

Google’s own handling of data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy, not by this one.

3.1 Installation and updates

Google Play tells us nothing that identifies you. We can see aggregate statistics in the Play Console, such as install counts, crash rates and country totals, and any review you choose to write publicly.

3.2 Leaderboards and achievements (Google Play Games Services)

Signing in is optional, and the game can be played without it. If you sign in, your best height is submitted to a public leaderboard hosted by Google, alongside your Play Games gamertag and avatar, and the game unlocks Play Games achievements for milestones you reach. Anyone viewing the leaderboard can see that gamertag, avatar and score, and your unlocked achievements are visible on your Play Games profile according to your Play Games settings. The game receives no email address and no real name, only the player identity Google provides for this game.

3.3 Cloud save (Play Games saved games)

If you are signed in, the game keeps one copy of your progress as a Play Games saved game, stored by Google in your own Google account, so a reinstall or a second device can pick it back up. It holds your progress, your pets and their names, your in-game balance and items, and what you have bought. It does not hold your settings.

We never see it. The game writes it straight to Google from your device and reads it back the same way. It is not shared with other players.

3.4 In-app purchases

Purchases are made through Google Play, which takes the payment. The game and we never see your card or payment details. What you bought (the product, and the transaction id of each bits pack) is kept in the save on your device and in your cloud save, which is the only backup a bits pack has. That is why buying needs a Play Games sign-in. Nothing about a purchase is sent anywhere else.

One-time products are restored through Google Play. Bits come back with the cloud save.

4. Crash diagnostics (Sentry)

When the game crashes or hits an unexpected error, a diagnostic report is sent to Sentry, which we use to find and fix bugs. A report contains the error and its stack trace, the app version, the device model, the operating system version, and technical context about what the app was doing. Sentry also receives the IP address the report arrived from as part of delivering it.

We use these reports only to diagnose faults in the game and do not try to identify players from them. They are kept for up to 90 days and then deleted.

5. What we never collect

The game does not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera input, or precise location. It has no analytics SDK and no advertising of any kind.

6. Children

Bit Pet Climber is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through this app, contact us at the address below and we will act on it.

You have the right to access, correct, erase and object to the processing of your personal data, and to complain to your national data protection authority. Because we hold no database of players, most of these rights are exercised through the device and Google account controls above, but write to us and we will help.

8. Changes to this policy

When the game gains a feature that changes what data is involved, this page is updated and the date at the top changes with it.
